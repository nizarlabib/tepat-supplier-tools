# DESIGN BRIEF — TEPAT SUPPLIER TOOLS

## 1. Brand Overview

**Company Name:** Tepat Supplier Tools

**Industry:** Industrial Tools & Machining Supplies

**Business Description:**

Tepat Supplier Tools is an established supplier specializing in lathe workshop equipment, machining tools, and industrial cutting tools.

The company provides various tools and equipment for:
- Lathe workshops.
- Machining operations.
- Industrial workshop activities.
- Cutting and drilling applications.

Main products include:
- Drill Bits / Mata Bor.
- Hand Tap.
- Tungsten Carbide Inserts / Insert Widia.
- NACHI Drill Bits.
- Other machining tools and workshop accessories.

**Target Audience:**
- Lathe workshop owners.
- Machinists and machining technicians.
- Industrial workshop operators.
- Businesses requiring machining equipment.
- Industrial procurement teams.

## 2. Website Objectives

The website is a corporate website and product information platform.

Primary objectives:
1. Establish company credibility and professionalism.
2. Introduce Tepat Supplier Tools to potential customers.
3. Showcase products and product categories.
4. Make it easy for visitors to discover available product types.
5. Generate inquiries through WhatsApp.

This is NOT a full e-commerce website.

Do not implement shopping cart, checkout, online payment, or inventory management.

## 3. Brand Personality

The brand should communicate:

- Professional.
- Reliable.
- Experienced.
- Modern.
- Industrial.
- Precise.
- Trustworthy.

The website should feel like an established industrial tools supplier that understands the needs of machining and lathe workshop customers.

Avoid making the brand look like a generic startup, consumer marketplace, or overly futuristic technology company.

## 4. Visual Direction

**Primary Design Style: MODERN INDUSTRIAL MINIMALIST**

The visual direction combines modern corporate design with industrial product presentation.

Design characteristics:
- White-dominant layouts.
- Light grey section backgrounds.
- Graphite typography.
- Industrial blue accents.
- Generous whitespace.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Clean product photography.
- Structured and balanced layouts.
- Subtle and meaningful interactions.

Avoid:
- Bronze and gold accents.
- Warm beige backgrounds.
- Excessive gradients.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Heavy shadows.
- Overly rounded components.
- Excessive decorative elements.
- Generic AI-generated website layouts.

## 5. Official Color Palette

Use the following color palette consistently throughout the website.

| Color | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | Primary background |
| Light Grey | #F3F5F7 | Alternate section background |
| Steel Grey | #E2E7EC | Borders and dividers |
| Graphite | #202A35 | Headings and primary text |
| Industrial Blue | #315F85 | Primary brand accent |
| Blue Accent | #2563EB | CTA buttons and interactive elements |

### Color Usage Guidelines

- White should be the dominant background color.
- Light grey should separate sections without creating visual clutter.
- Graphite should be used for headings and body text.
- Industrial blue should communicate brand identity.
- Bright blue should be reserved for primary interactions and important CTAs.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast and readability.

Never introduce bronze, gold, or warm beige as alternative brand colors without explicit approval.

## 6. Typography

Recommended typography:

**Headings:** Manrope

**Body:** Inter

**Supporting Text:** Inter

Typography guidelines:
- Strong heading hierarchy.
- Clear and readable body text.
- Appropriate line heights.
- Controlled heading widths.
- Consistent font weights.
- Responsive font sizes.

Avoid unnecessarily decorative typography.

## 7. Layout and Design System

General layout principles:

- Consistent content width.
- Generous horizontal and vertical spacing.
- Balanced section proportions.
- Consistent grid systems.
- Clean alignment.
- Subtle borders.
- Minimal shadows.
- Consistent button sizes and interaction states.

Avoid using the same card layout for every section.

Use different compositions where appropriate:
- Split layouts.
- Editorial layouts.
- Asymmetric grids.
- Full-width photography.
- Product showcase grids.
- Typography-led sections.

## 8. Photography and Visual Assets

Prioritize authentic industrial product photography.

Preferred subjects:
- Drill bits.
- Hand taps.
- Tungsten carbide inserts.
- Machining accessories.
- Lathe workshop tools.
- Industrial cutting tools.

Image guidelines:
- Clean backgrounds.
- Sharp product details.
- Consistent image proportions.
- Professional lighting.
- High-quality product presentation.

Avoid unrelated office photography, generic business meeting images, and decorative illustrations.

Use company-owned, licensed, or otherwise authorized images.

If official assets are unavailable, use clearly identified placeholders.

## 9. Website Structure

### A. Navbar

Include:
- Company logo.
- Beranda.
- Tentang Kami.
- Produk.
- Kontak.
- WhatsApp CTA.

Design:
- Clean white background.
- Graphite text.
- Minimal navigation.
- Industrial blue CTA.

### B. Hero Section

Primary headline direction:

"Solusi Perlengkapan Bengkel Bubut dan Kebutuhan Machining."

Supporting message:

"Menyediakan berbagai kebutuhan peralatan machining dan perkakas industri untuk mendukung pekerjaan bengkel bubut secara profesional."

CTA:
- Jelajahi Produk.
- Hubungi Kami.

Visual:
Use premium industrial product photography.

### C. About Section

Introduce Tepat Supplier Tools as an established industrial tools supplier.

Highlight:
- Company experience.
- Product variety.
- Understanding of machining needs.
- Commitment to serving workshop customers.

Do not invent the company's founding year or unsupported achievements.

### D. Product Showcase

Introduce the main product categories:

- Mata Bor / Drill Bits.
- Hand Tap.
- Insert Widia.
- Bor NACHI.
- Aksesoris Machining.

Use simple product cards containing:
- Product image.
- Product name.
- Brand where available.
- Short description.

The dedicated product page should be a simple product information catalog, not a marketplace.

### E. Why Choose Us

Highlight the company's relevant strengths.

Use factual information supplied by the company.

Avoid unsupported claims, invented numbers, certifications, and distributor relationships.

### F. Contact Section

Encourage visitors to:
- Ask about product availability.
- Request product recommendations.
- Request quotations.
- Contact the company through WhatsApp.

### G. Footer

Include:
- Company identity.
- Navigation.
- Contact information.
- Address.
- Social media links.

Use placeholders until official information is supplied.

## 10. Responsive Design

Support:
- Desktop.
- Tablet.
- Mobile.

Requirements:
- Responsive navigation.
- Readable typography.
- Consistent spacing.
- Adaptive product grids.
- Mobile-friendly CTA buttons.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Accessible interactive elements.

## 11. Technical Design Constraints

Existing technology stack:
- Vite.
- React.
- TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS v4.
- Lucide React.
- Motion when necessary.

Development principles:
- Reusable components.
- Maintainable folder structure.
- Semantic HTML.
- Accessible interactions.
- Minimal dependencies.
- Consistent design tokens.

Avoid unnecessary abstractions and overengineering.

## 12. Content and Brand Accuracy

Important requirements:

- Do not invent company history.
- Do not fabricate product specifications.
- Do not invent customer testimonials.
- Do not invent partnerships or official distributor status.
- Do not claim authorized NACHI distributor status unless verified.
- Do not use unlicensed logos or photography.
- Use clearly identified placeholders for missing information.

## 13. Overall Design Goal

The final website should communicate:

"An experienced, reliable, and professional supplier of machining tools and lathe workshop equipment, presented through a modern industrial corporate identity."

The website should feel clean, trustworthy, modern, and established.

The visual identity must remain consistent across all pages, including the homepage, product catalog, and contact section.
